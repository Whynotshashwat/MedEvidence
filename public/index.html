Welcome to MedEvidence

Here's how to evaluate the CooL SDK cryptographic evidence demo

1

Create a Patient

Click Patients in the sidebar → New Patient → enter any MRN, name, DOB, and sex.

2

Run AI Triage

Open the patient → New Case → enter vitals (e.g. HR: 72, RR: 16, Temp: 37.0, BP: 120) → Submit. The CooL SDK creates a cryptographic evidence receipt.

3

Verify Evidence

Open the case → click Verify. See CooL's cryptographic verification results. Expand Coded Receipt to inspect the full signed evidence.

4

Simulate Tamper

Click Tamper to mutate the stored data → click Verify again. The binding hash check fails, proving tamper detection works. Then click Revert to restore original data and verify again.
